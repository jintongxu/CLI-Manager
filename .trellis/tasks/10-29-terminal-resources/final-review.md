## Confirmed findings and fixes

1. **Per-session enumeration cap was exceeded (medium).** `job_resources.rs::members` previously allowed three queries independently for initial and final membership checks, permitting six calls for one session despite the PRD's three-attempt session cap. The existing churn test explicitly expected four queries after two initial retries. Initial/final enumeration now share a request-local session counter (`job_resources.rs:45–62,74–76,89`). Without a final-check permit, valid observations remain a partial subtotal with `membership_changed`, never complete. Regression expands the production-aggregator churn test: initial retry exhaustion, final retry success, and final retry exhaustion all stop at three queries.
2. **CSS-hidden resources kept polling (medium).** `TerminalTabsView` hides the whole terminal well with `display:none` during history, and `workspace-layout.css:54–57` hides mounted panel frames when `terminalSidePanelVisible=false`. Neither condition reached the resources activity gate: independent composition omitted `visible`; merged composition checked only the selected side-panel tab. Added `visible={terminalSidePanelVisible && !historyActive}` for independent resources and forwarded the same condition via `TerminalSidePanel.resourcesVisible` for merged resources (`TerminalTabsView.tsx:492,565`, `TerminalSidePanel.tsx:194`). Existing open/tab/document gates, generation invalidation and deferred singleflight remain unchanged. New regression reads actual TSX attribute expressions and covers both compositions × panel shown/hidden × history active/inactive, including inactive merged tabs.
